## Red Flags & Dispute Talking Points

### 1. IMEA Number Error

- **Flag:** Tech support stated hotspot IMEA was "incorrectly flagged in system" and "had been in system for a long time"
- **Impact:** Likely caused hotspot to be billed as personal phone, forcing it out of 3-line bundle into premium tier
- **Dispute:** This is a **system error**, not customer action. AT&T should correct retroactively.

### 2. No Notification of Plan Changes

- **Flag:** Bill shows plan names changing from "Starter SL - 3 Lines" to "AT&T 55+ 2 Lines" to "Premium PL - 1 Line" with no written notice
- **Dispute:** Industry standard requires email/letter notification before material plan changes. Silence is admission of unauthorized change.

### 3. Loyalty Discount Vanished on Michael's Line

- **Flag:** June 2025 shows -$5 loyalty discount on Michael's line. August 2026 shows $0 loyalty discount.
- **Dispute:** Loyalty discount should have continued (26-year customer). This is an unauthorized removal.

### 4. Hotspot Usage = Zero

- **Flag:** Hotspot used 0.00 GB in Aug billing period, yet charges $92.26/month
- **Dispute:** If hotspot was underutilized and billing escalated due to IMEA error, usage history can support retroactive plan correction.

### 5. Apple Financing Lacks Documentation

- **Flag:** Apple 128GB financing ($479.99 × 2 lines) established Jan 11, 2025, but no authorization evidence in available records
- **Dispute:** If financing was unauthorized, can it be cancelled with refund of payments made to date?
