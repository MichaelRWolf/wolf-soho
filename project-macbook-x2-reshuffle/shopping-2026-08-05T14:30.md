## eBay Refurbished: Evaluation Criteria

### What Makes a Refurbished Machine Worth It?

**Seller Quality:**

- Rating >97% with >5K feedback = proven track record
- wireless-source: 97.5% positive, 24.1K ratings ✅ Top-tier

**Condition Grades (eBay standard):**

- **Excellent:** Minor signs of use; fully functional; cosmetic near-perfect
- **Very Good:** Minor cosmetic wear; fully functional; acceptable refurb baseline
- **Good:** Visible wear; functional; risky if price doesn't justify
- **Fair:** Heavy wear or defects; generally avoid

**Returns & Warranty:**

- Free 30-day returns = safety net ✅ (both options have this)
- 1-year warranty = better; none = higher risk

**Price Validation:**

- Compare to Pre-Owned baseline from shopping-2026-08-05T12:30.md
- Refurb should be 10-25% cheaper than equivalent Pre-Owned
- If refurb > Pre-Owned, question the value

**Battery Health:**

- Not visible in listing; must ask seller before purchase
- Excellent refurb: <300 cycles (like new)
- Good refurb: 300-700 cycles (acceptable)
- Avoid: >800 cycles (used hard)
