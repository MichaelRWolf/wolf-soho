## Problem

Listed as "Apple MFi Certified iPhone Headphones **Wired** with Lightning Connector 2 Pack" (seller: Matsusho, $9.99). On plug-in, iPhone shows a Beats/AirPods-style pairing popup and requires Bluetooth pairing. Return window closed Oct 3, 2026.

## Observations

- Plugging into Lightning triggers a Bluetooth pairing popup (Beats-style card).
- Device appears in Bluetooth settings named "Lightning."
- Forgetting old Beats devices did not stop the popup.
- Prior wired Lightning earbuds (past decade) never required pairing.

## Hypothesis

The Lightning plug contains a Bluetooth radio. The port only supplies power, and the audio travels over Bluetooth. The chip imitates Apple's quick-pair signal, which is why the Beats-style card appears. Genuine MFi wired Lightning headsets don't pair, so the "Wired" and "MFi Certified" claims are misrepresentations, which grounds an "item not as described" claim.
