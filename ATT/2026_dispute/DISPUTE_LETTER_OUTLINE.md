- This rate was presented as our negotiated "loyal customer" rate after discussion with store staff.

**Evidence:**

- Wendy's line: $65.00

**Baseline expectation:** Bills should remain stable at or near this negotiated level.

**Our position:** This is a system error on AT&T's part. We should not bear the cost of AT&T's internal classification mistake.
