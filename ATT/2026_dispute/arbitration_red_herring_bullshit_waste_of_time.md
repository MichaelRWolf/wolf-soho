# Arbitration Red Herring (Discussion Capture, 2026-11-07)

## Context

AT&T's Consumer Service Agreement changes take effect 2026-11-01. The 2026-10-24 bill (page 4) states that continued use means acceptance. The account total is $267.98 (Aug 2026) and $246.65 (Oct 2026). The disputed amount is under $2,000 on a single account.

## Discussion

1. **Comparing old and new arbitration terms** is a trivial exercise. The summary changes are the administrator (AAA to JAMS), mass-claim routing to court after 25+ similar claims, and class and jury waivers extended to court. A line-by-line comparison adds nothing to the claim.
2. **Comparing AT&T's arbitration process to other carriers** is a waste of time. Other major carriers have similar clauses, and the comparison does not change the available options for this account.
3. **Investigating arbitration for a claim under $2,000 on one account** is out of proportion. The cost of research and paperwork exceeds the amount in dispute. Arbitration rules matter for class-scale claims, not single-account claims.

## Connection to the two goals

- **Goal 1 (Cheboygan changes reflected on the bill):** No connection. Arbitration terms do not affect whether the bill shows the October 2026 changes. Confirmation depends on the 2026-11-24 bill.
- **Goal 2 (customer service account review request filed):** No connection. Goal 2 is a customer service request, not an arbitration matter. The arbitration terms do not affect the account review request.

## Path forward

- No formal dispute stage has been reached, so arbitration is not in play.
- The smaller path is customer service, account review, and customer retention. Start there.
- Stop further research on arbitration terms and other carriers for this claim.
